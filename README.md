Absolutely. I think WCBN should not simply be a business club for WCA members. It should become a selective community of Christian business leaders whose businesses, leadership and influence are capable of advancing the WCA mandate and producing measurable positive impact in the world.

I reviewed the current WCA website and the UN SDG framework. WCA describes its vision around building fellowships that are “spiritually, intellectually, and economically empowered to bring positive change,” while its mission is “Win, Train, Transform.” The organization also explicitly emphasizes capacity building, accountability and integrity. (World Changers Association)

That gives us a very strong foundation for WCBN.

Proposed WCBN Membership Validation Framework

1. The fundamental principle

I would define the relationship like this:

WCA membership is a prerequisite for WCBN membership, but WCA membership does not automatically qualify a person for WCBN membership.

In other words:

WCA → Spiritual/Leadership Foundation

WCBN → Business/Impact Expression of that Foundation

So WCBN should consist of people who can demonstrate:

Faith + Character + Business Capacity + Leadership + Impact + Alignment

The ultimate question should not merely be:

“Is this person a Christian who owns a business?”

It should be:

“Is this a person whose business, leadership and influence can contribute to changing the world in alignment with the WCA vision?”

2. The WCBN Membership Philosophy

I would propose that WCBN adopt this statement:

WCBN exists to identify, connect, equip and mobilize Christian business leaders whose enterprises and influence can create economic, social and spiritual transformation in alignment with the vision and values of WCA.

This is important because it prevents WCBN from becoming simply:

a networking club

a Christian entrepreneurs' WhatsApp group

a business referral association

a chamber of commerce

a marketplace for WCA members

Instead, it becomes a transformation network.

3. The six pillars of WCBN membership

Every applicant should be assessed against six pillars.

PillarCore Question1. WCA AlignmentIs the person genuinely part of WCA and aligned with its mission?2. Christian CharacterDoes the person's character reflect Christian values?3. Business CapacityDoes the person have a legitimate business, profession or economic mandate?4. Leadership & InfluenceDoes the person demonstrate leadership and the ability to influence others positively?5. Global ImpactIs the person creating or capable of creating meaningful social/economic/environmental impact?6. SDG AlignmentDoes the person's business or initiatives contribute to one or more UN Sustainable Development Goals?

The UN framework contains 17 SDGs and 169 associated targets, covering areas ranging from poverty, health and education to economic growth, innovation, climate, institutions and partnerships. (Sustainable Development Goals)

But I would not make all 17 SDGs mandatory.

That would make the process artificial.

Instead:

Every WCBN member should identify at least one SDG to which their business or professional influence makes a meaningful contribution, with preference given to members contributing to multiple goals.

4. The most important distinction: Eligibility vs Selection

This is where I think WCBN can become very sophisticated.

Don't make the application a simple checklist.

Create two levels.

LEVEL 1 — Eligibility

The applicant must satisfy the minimum requirements.

LEVEL 2 — Selection

Among eligible applicants, WCBN determines whether the individual is actually suitable for the network.

This allows you to say:

“You are a WCA member and therefore eligible to apply, but WCBN membership is selective.”

5. Stage 1 — WCA Membership Verification

This should be an automatic gate.

Question:

Is the applicant an active WCA member?

Verify:

WCA membership ID

WCA chapter/DCG

date joined WCA

current membership status

local leader/pastor

participation/standing where appropriate

Decision

NO → Application rejected/ineligible

YES → Proceed

I would actually make this the first question in the application.

“Are you currently a registered member of WCA?”

The system can then request the WCA membership number.

This prevents WCBN from becoming an alternative route into WCA.

6. Stage 2 — Christian Character & WCA Alignment

This should be more than asking:

“Are you a Christian?”

That question is too weak.

Instead, evaluate evidence of Christian character.

Suggested assessment areas

A. Faith commitment

Christian faith

understanding of Christian values

personal commitment to Christ

willingness to uphold WCA's Christian identity

B. Integrity

honesty

financial integrity

truthfulness

ethical conduct

transparency

C. Stewardship

attitude toward money

treatment of employees

treatment of customers

responsibility toward society

D. Servant leadership

willingness to serve

develops others

contributes beyond personal gain

E. Reputation

standing within WCA

leadership recommendation

known ethical concerns

Critical principle

Don't attempt to judge someone's salvation.

WCBN is not a spiritual tribunal.

Instead, evaluate whether the observable conduct of the applicant is reasonably consistent with the Christian values WCBN claims to uphold.

7. Stage 3 — Business Validation

The person should demonstrate a legitimate economic activity.

But I would make WCBN broader than simply “business owners.”

Potential categories:

Founder / Entrepreneur

Owns or leads a business.

Executive / Corporate Leader

Leads a significant organization or business unit.

Professional

Doctor, lawyer, engineer, consultant, accountant, architect, etc.

Investor

Invests capital into productive enterprises.

Social Entrepreneur

Uses enterprise to solve social problems.

Business/Marketplace Leader

Has significant economic influence even without owning a company.

This allows WCBN to build a business ecosystem, rather than just a collection of entrepreneurs.

8. Stage 4 — Business Integrity Due Diligence

This is extremely important.

Because WCBN membership is effectively an endorsement by association.

A member's actions can damage WCBN and WCA.

Therefore applicants should declare:

legal name of business

business registration where applicable

sector

products/services

country of operation

years operating

number of employees

website/social media

major customers/markets

ownership/leadership position

regulatory licenses where applicable

major litigation/regulatory issues

conflicts of interest

whether the business operates in restricted/high-risk sectors

You don't necessarily need a full corporate investigation for every applicant.

Instead, establish risk levels.

Green

Normal business.

→ Standard validation.

Amber

Some concerns or incomplete information.

→ Additional review.

Red

Serious ethical/legal/reputational concern.

→ Escalate to WCBN leadership committee.

This is where I would make WCBN different from ordinary Christian business networks.

Instead of asking:

“Which SDG do you like?”

The UN itself describes the SDGs as interconnected rather than isolated goals. (United Nations)

Therefore WCBN should focus on actual contribution, not simply claiming an SDG.

10. Introduce an “Impact Commitment”

This could become one of the most powerful elements of WCBN.

Every applicant should answer:

“What change do you intend your business or professional influence to create in the next 3–5 years?”

Then:

“Which SDG(s) does this contribution advance?”

And:

“What measurable evidence will demonstrate your contribution?”

For example:

“Our company intends to train 10,000 young people in digital skills within five years.”

That maps strongly to:

SDG 4 — Quality Education

and potentially:

SDG 8 — Decent Work & Economic Growth.

Another person might say:

“We intend to provide affordable solar energy to 50,000 households.”

That's:

SDG 7 + SDG 11 + SDG 13.

Now WCBN isn't just collecting businesses.

It is building an impact portfolio.

11. Stage 6 — Leadership Assessment

This is critical because WCBN is not merely about business ownership.

Assess:

Vision

Does the applicant think beyond personal prosperity?

Influence

Does the applicant influence employees, customers, communities or industries?

Execution

Does the applicant actually execute ideas?

Development of people

Does the applicant create opportunities for others?

Collaboration

Can the applicant work with other leaders?

Global mindset

Does the applicant think beyond their immediate environment?

12. Stage 7 — WCBN Interview

I recommend a 30–45 minute membership interview.

Not a job interview.

It should be a values + vision + impact conversation.

Suggested questions:

Faith

What does your faith mean to you as a business leader?

How does your Christian worldview influence your decisions?

Business

What problem does your business solve?

Why did you start it?

What is your biggest business challenge?

Leadership

Who are you intentionally developing?

How do you handle ethical dilemmas?

Impact

What change do you want your business to create?

Which SDG(s) does your work contribute to?

What measurable impact do you expect over the next 3–5 years?

WCA/WCBN

Why do you want to join WCBN?

What do you believe you can contribute to WCBN?

What would you like WCBN to help you accomplish?

That last question is extremely important.

You don't want people joining solely to receive.

You want people who will contribute.

13. Stage 8 — Reference / Sponsorship

I strongly recommend requiring one WCA leader endorsement.

Potentially:

Applicant → WCA Leader/Chapter Leader → WCBN

The leader confirms:

“To the best of my knowledge, this individual is an active member of WCA and demonstrates conduct reasonably consistent with WCA's values.”

For higher-risk applicants, require two references.

I recommend:

Proceed to admission.

Proceed, potentially with conditions.

DEFERRED

Below 60

NOT RECOMMENDED

But there should also be automatic disqualification conditions.

16. Automatic disqualification

Regardless of score:

Immediate rejection / escalation for:

serious financial fraud

proven corruption

serious criminal conduct relevant to membership

deliberate deception in application

serious abuse/exploitation

discriminatory or hateful business practices

significant unresolved ethical violations

business activities fundamentally incompatible with WCBN's values

And importantly:

An applicant should never be able to compensate for serious character problems with business success.

Someone shouldn't get 95/100 because they have a $10 million company if they fundamentally violate WCBN's values.

18. I would add one more thing: WCBN Membership should be a covenant

This is particularly important because of the Christian identity of WCA.

Upon admission, the person should not simply click:

“I accept the terms.”

They should make a WCBN Leadership & Business Covenant.

Something along the lines of:

As a member of the World Changers Business Network, I commit to conducting my business and professional affairs with integrity, excellence, stewardship and respect for others; to pursue legitimate economic prosperity without compromising Christian values; to use my influence and resources to create positive transformation; to support the development of others; to pursue opportunities that contribute to sustainable human and economic development; and to uphold the vision, values and reputation of WCA and WCBN.

This turns membership into a commitment, not merely an accreditation.

19. Annual revalidation

This is another major distinction I recommend.

Membership should not be “approved once and forever.”

Every year, members complete a short:

WCBN Annual Impact Review

They report:

business status

major achievements

people employed

people trained/mentored

community initiatives

SDG contribution

partnerships created

challenges

next year's impact objectives

Then WCBN can calculate:

WCBN Global Impact Index

For example:

Members: 500
Countries: 12
Businesses: 430
Jobs created: 8,400
People trained: 25,000
Businesses funded: 1,200
SDGs addressed: 13
Community initiatives: 180

Now WCBN can eventually publish an annual:

WCBN Global Transformation Report

That would be extremely powerful.

20. I would also create WCBN membership categories

Rather than everyone being treated exactly the same, consider:

WCBN Associate

New/inexperienced business professional with demonstrated potential.

WCBN Member

Fully validated business/professional member.

WCBN Leader

Demonstrated leadership and significant contribution.

WCBN Impact Partner

An individual/business with significant measurable contribution to WCBN's transformation agenda.

WCBN Fellow

Exceptional leaders whose influence and contribution have demonstrated significant alignment with WCA's global mission.

This gives members a path of progression.

21. The strategic model I recommend

Ultimately, I would position WCBN around this equation:

WCA + BUSINESS + CHRISTIAN CHARACTER + IMPACT = WCBN

Or even more powerfully:

WIN → BUILD → TRANSFORM → IMPACT

Where:

WIN
Bring people into the WCA family.

BUILD
Develop their spiritual, intellectual and economic capacity.

TRANSFORM
Help them become effective leaders and kingdom-minded business builders.

IMPACT
Mobilize their businesses, resources and influence to transform communities and nations.

This fits exceptionally well with the WCA's existing “Win, Train, Transform” mission and its emphasis on spiritual and professional development, capacity building, accountability and integrity. (World Changers Association)

22. One important adjustment to the SDG idea

I would not say that WCBN membership requires someone to be “working on the SDGs.”

That makes WCBN sound like a UN-affiliated organization.

Instead say:

“WCBN recognizes the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals as a global framework for identifying and measuring areas of positive human, economic and environmental impact.”

Then WCBN can say:

“Every WCBN member is expected to identify at least one area of measurable impact aligned with one or more SDGs.”

This preserves WCBN's Christian identity and WCA authority, while giving it a globally recognized impact framework.

The SDGs themselves are intended as a universal framework addressing economic growth, social well-being, inequality, environmental protection and global partnerships. (United Nations)

23. The resulting WCBN admission standard

I would summarize the entire philosophy in one sentence:

WCBN membership is granted to WCA members who demonstrate Christian character, professional or business competence, leadership potential, ethical integrity and a credible commitment to creating measurable positive impact in society.

And the five questions the validation committee should ultimately answer are:

1. Is he/she one of us?

WCA membership and alignment.

2. Can we trust him/her?

Christian character and integrity.

3. Can he/she build?

Business/professional competence and execution.

4. Can he/she influence?

Leadership and network contribution.

5. Can he/she change something?

Impact, innovation and SDG alignment.

If the answer to all five is YES, you have a strong WCBN candidate.
