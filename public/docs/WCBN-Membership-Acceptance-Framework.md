# WCBN Membership Acceptance Framework

**World Changers Business Network (WCBN)** — a subsidiary of the World Changers Association (WCA)

This document summarises the framework for accepting businesses and professionals into WCBN membership.

---

## 1. Membership Tracks

WCBN admits two types of members, each with its own application, review path, and portal experience:

| Track | Who it is for |
|---|---|
| **Business owner** | Members who own or co-own a registered business. |
| **Professional** | Members who practise a profession or trade without a registered business (employed, self-employed, consultants, practitioners). |

The applicant chooses a track at the start of the application. The choice is locked once the application is submitted; an administrator can return an application if the wrong track was chosen.

---

## 2. Universal Eligibility (Both Tracks)

Every applicant, regardless of track, must satisfy the WCA identity requirements, which are **verified automatically** from the shared WCA database:

1. **Active WCA membership** — the applicant must be an active member of the World Changers Association.
2. **Active DCG participation** — the applicant must belong to an active Discipleship Cell Group (DCG).
3. **Covenant acceptance** — the applicant must confirm and accept the WCBN covenant.

No separate sign-up exists on the WCBN platform; members use their existing WCA credentials.

---

## 3. Application Requirements

### 3.1 Business Track

- Business name, sector, country and cities of operation
- Founding year and team size
- Business phone and email
- Registration references
- Description of what the business does
- Impact commitment (how the business serves people and reflects Kingdom values)
- Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) the business advances

### 3.2 Professional Track

- Profession / title and field of practice
- Practice type (employed, self-employed, consultant, public service, ministry, student, other)
- Country and city
- Years of experience
- Qualifications and certifications
- Professional body / licence reference (optional)
- Work phone and email
- Description of the work they do
- How their work serves people and reflects Kingdom values
- Career and service goals
- SDGs their work advances
- Optional portfolio or LinkedIn link

Applications can be saved as drafts and completed progressively before submission.

---

## 4. Review & Validation Process

Each application moves through a staged review pipeline managed from the WCBN admin portal. Stages differ by track:

| Stage | Business | Professional |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| Applied | ✓ | ✓ |
| WCA verified | ✓ | ✓ |
| Character review | ✓ | ✓ |
| Business review | ✓ | — |
| Professional practice review | — | ✓ |
| Impact & SDG review / Service & impact review | ✓ | ✓ |
| Leadership review | ✓ | ✓ |
| Interview | ✓ | ✓ |
| Committee review | ✓ | ✓ |
| Decision | ✓ | ✓ |
| Inducted | ✓ | ✓ |

Applicants can follow their progress through a review tracker in their member portal. While an application is being processed, the portal shows only the verification status, the review tracker, and a summary of the submitted application.

---

## 5. Scoring Criteria (Admin-Configurable)

All acceptance criteria are configurable by WCBN leadership from the admin portal — no code changes required:

- **Criteria per track** — each criterion is marked as applying to businesses, professionals, or both.
- **Weights and scores** — each criterion carries a weight; reviewers score applications against them.
- **Thresholds** — each track has its own *passing score* and *strong candidate score*.
- **Versioning** — criteria are versioned; applications in flight keep the rules they were submitted under.
- **Required documents, review stages and disqualification rules** are also admin-configurable.

---

## 6. Approval & Activation

- Approval sets the member's type (business or professional), which determines their portal experience.
- Only **validated and activated** members see the full member portal (overview, contributions, impact, covenant, events, news, profile).
- Business members manage a business listing; professional members manage a professional profile. Both are vetted and activated separately before appearing in the public directory.
- The public catalog shows vetted, activated listings in two tabs — **Businesses** and **Professionals** — each with a public detail page.

---

## 7. Ongoing Membership Obligations

- **Contributions** — annual/monthly dues with declared payments and admin confirmation.
- **Impact reporting** — members maintain impact commitments and SDG reporting.
- **Covenant standing** — continued adherence to the WCBN covenant.
- **Periodic review** — membership standing is reviewed on a scheduled basis.
